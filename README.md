# Amir Karimi
 
I build infrastructure, backend, and AI systems that have to remain reliable under real traffic, operational complexity, and imperfect requirements.
 
Over the past eight years I have worked on high-traffic products serving 50M+ users, led backend engineers, improved production throughput, and built systems across distributed services, developer infrastructure, and applied AI. Most recently that meant a centralised LLM gateway handling 400K+ requests/day with per-team attribution, quotas and cost governance, and an event-sourced AWS provisioning platform that automated isolated environments for internal product launches.
 
My public work includes merged contributions to Django and LangChainGo. Most of my recent engineering work lives in private company repositories, so the projects below are selected examples of how I approach architecture, performance, reliability, and implementation.
